# Widget Service overview

The Widget Service stores widgets and serves them over HTTP. As of this writing,
BigQuery doesn't support the export function. You can share services between
multiple clients. The codebase assumes Big Endian byte ordering.

## Behavior

The service is queried by you, and an acknowledgment is sent by the server.
You can send an unsubscribe message. The server would then remove you from the
mailing list. Windows isn't currently supported.

Your app might experience instability, high latency, and so on. Analyze the
businesses' data before you raise the quota.
